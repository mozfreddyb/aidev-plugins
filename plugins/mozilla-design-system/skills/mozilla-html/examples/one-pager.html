Two sites, one window

Split view puts any two tabs side by side, so you can compare, copy, and keep an eye on things without juggling windows.

Get Firefox See how it works

How it works

Pick two tabs

Right-click a tab and choose “Open in split view”.

Resize the divide

Drag the handle between the pages to give either side more room.

Close either side

The other tab returns to the tab strip where you left it.
